# ATAC-seq

## Definition
> **ATAC-seq** (Assay for Transposase-Accessible Chromatin using sequencing) profiles genome-wide chromatin accessibility. The single-cell variant (**scATAC-seq**) reveals regulatory element activity at single-cell resolution, complementing transcriptomic data from scRNA-seq.

## Key Ideas
- Measures open chromatin regions → identifies active regulatory elements (promoters, enhancers)
- Single-cell resolution enables cell-type-specific regulatory landscape mapping
- Multi-modal pairing with scRNA-seq links regulatory elements to gene expression
