# Tabula Muris

## Overview
> A compendium of ~100K single-cell transcriptomic profiles from 20 mouse organs and tissues, generated by the Tabula Muris Consortium. Provides both FACS-sorted (Smart-seq2) and droplet-based (10x) data.

## Usage
- Benchmark for reconstruction and generation quality in [[arixv 2025 Scalable Single-Cell Gene Expression Generation with Latent Diffusion Models|scLDM]]
- Cross-tissue cell type annotation benchmark
