# Human Lung Cell Atlas (HLCA)

## Overview
> An integrated single-cell atlas of the human lung, combining data from multiple studies. Covers diverse cell types across healthy and diseased lung tissue.

## Usage
- Reconstruction benchmark in [[arixv 2025 Scalable Single-Cell Gene Expression Generation with Latent Diffusion Models|scLDM]]
- Reference atlas for lung disease studies

## Source and scope

[Sikkema et al., Nature Medicine (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02327-2) reports the integrated atlas: 49 datasets, over 2.4 million cells and 486 individuals. The healthy reference core and mapped disease extension are distinct objects; benchmark subsets need their own counts. Checked 2026-10-20.
