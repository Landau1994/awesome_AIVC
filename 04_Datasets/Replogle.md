# Replogle

## Overview
> A genome-scale Perturb-seq dataset (Replogle et al., 2022) profiling CRISPR gene knockouts across thousands of genes in K562 cells. One of the largest single-cell perturbation screens available.

## Usage
- Perturbational benchmark in [[arixv 2025 Scalable Single-Cell Gene Expression Generation with Latent Diffusion Models|scLDM]], [[GEARS]], [[CPA]]
- Standard testbed for perturbation prediction models
