# COVID-19 Lung Atlas

## Overview
> Single-cell atlas of COVID-19 patient lung tissue, capturing immune and epithelial cell responses to SARS-CoV-2 infection. Used as a downstream classification benchmark for foundation models.

## Usage
- Downstream classification benchmark in [[arixv 2025 Scalable Single-Cell Gene Expression Generation with Latent Diffusion Models|scLDM]] (F1=0.820 with 270M model)
- Disease-specific cell type annotation evaluation
